— Meet the founder

Built by someone who knows health, performance & growth from the inside.

Kinsey Middleton is the founder of Kin-Aesthetics and a former professional marathon runner who represented Canada at the IAAF World Championships and won the 2018 Canadian National Marathon Championship.

Her background in elite sport shaped how she thinks about performance, discipline, systems, and health.

After more than a decade leading growth for brands across longevity, telehealth, functional medicine, concierge medicine, and health media, Kinsey founded Kin-Aesthetics to support the health companies raising the standard for what care can look like.

Today, she partners with founders, operators, and executive teams who need both strategic leadership and the right specialists around the table.

— FAQ

Questions decision-makers ask first.

What kinds of health and wellness brands do you work with?

Health, wellness, longevity, telehealth, functional medicine, concierge medicine, aesthetics, specialty care, health media, and patient-facing wellness brands. If your customer is making a real decision about their body or their health, we're probably a fit.

How are you different from a generalist growth agency?

We already understand the health category — trust-building, compliance-sensitive messaging, patient education, and the decision process behind high-consideration care. You skip the 6-month ramp.

Can you handle compliance-sensitive marketing?

Yes. We are not a legal or medical review team, but we build with compliance realities in mind from the beginning across paid media, website copy, content, and funnels — and we partner cleanly with your in-house reviewers.

What does a typical engagement look like?

Most engagements begin with a strategy and audit phase, followed by a roadmap and execution across the channels or systems that will move the business forward fastest. Engagements typically run 6–12 months.

Do you work with brands outside of Boise?

We're based in Boise, Idaho and work with founders and operators across North America. Most of our engagements are remote with periodic on-sites when it makes sense.
